## Per-video deal overrides (mixed talking/non-talking creators)

Only 3 exist, all on **bradyboosts** — three talking videos (posted Jun 20, Jun 26, Jun 29) pinned at the old talking terms $1.00 @ $100. All three predate Jul 20, so they stay historically correct and are unaffected by the restructure.

**How overrides interact with the new policy:** a per-video override always wins over the creator's dated deal and is *not* date-aware — it pins that one video's terms permanently. Going forward, when a non-talking creator posts a **talking video on/after Jul 20**, the override to enter is the *new* talking terms: **$1.00 CPM, $300 cap** (not $1 @ $100). The reverse case (talking creator posts a non-talking video) should be overridden to **$0.50 CPM, $100 cap**.

Verified visually in the live dashboard (creator portals, July 1–31): brady's videos posted ≤ Jul 19 price at $0.50 CPM with the $300 cap and videos ≥ Jul 20 at $0.50 with the $100 cap; Adam's at $0.75/$350 through Jul 19 (his 2.6M-view Jul 10 video correctly hits the $350 cap at $346.04) and $0.50/$100 from Jul 20.
