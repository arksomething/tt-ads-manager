## Definitions

- **Ever observed:** tracked videos with at least one complete direct metric observation / all tracked videos. Imported inventory alone does not count.
- **Current metrics:** non-frozen, not currently classified unavailable videos that have a complete direct observation and are not overdue under the worker cadence / videos in that same eligible set. The worker uses a 20-minute scheduler grace. A sample can be fresh despite a later failed attempt; this metric is not error-free collection.
- **Frozen:** videos past the 90-day tracking horizon; excluded from current metrics, retained in ever-observed and historical cutoff denominators.
- **Unavailable:** active videos excluded from current-metric percentage because of current explicit account state or latest unavailable-video evidence. This never means zero owed. Unknown/transient outcomes can still be operationally overdue.
- **Seven-day finalized:** videos with final status under the owned direct 168-hour policy (three-hour tolerance) / stored videos with a known publication time at least seven days old. This is historical owned-collector evidence only, not a payout approval rate; older imported videos often predate owned collection and may have separate Viral.app evidence not evaluated here. Videos with unknown publication dates are listed separately.
- **State:** latest account classification; ready does not mean every video is current or payout-ready. N/A is not 100%.
