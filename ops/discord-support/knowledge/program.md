# Creator program and resources

Reviewed 2026-09-14. These are agreed workflow rules; confirm the creator's cohort,
agreement and actual deployed server state before describing a feature as live.

## New versus legacy

At migration, participating owners of creator channels are legacy. Membership or
old roles alone do not establish participation. People without creator channels
are excluded from that migration roster, not automatically kicked from Discord.
Existing creators retain their deals/rates and day-to-day arrangements, including
those already onboarding at cutover. Legacy creators cannot re-onboard to obtain
the new rate. New Deal persists after trial success. Inactive legacy creators keep
their cohort, but Evan decides whether to restore participation; do not automatically
assign active status or Scripts on return. Never identify cohort from a guessed name.

## First publication and trial

New creators complete the applicable onboarding, account, warm-up, agreement and
initial draft-review steps. First video posted confirmation records the current
time; do not ask them to type a publication timestamp. This begins the seven-day
(168-hour) trial, not joining, signing or finishing warm-up. Scripts and New Deal
are automatically granted after that first publication confirmation.

The current review team receives a preliminary creator-performance report and reminder 24 hours before
the trial deadline to decide continuation. Missing tracking data is not zero
performance. Hide trial timing for full-time creators. Passing trial does not remove
New Deal. Do not invent automatic pass/fail thresholds or a rate from this handbook.
The workflow document says time off does not pause the trial; verify applicable
agreement if a creator disputes timing. Existing signed agreements are not rewritten.

## Scripts and review

Use the Scripts role, not a hardcoded list of recipients. Legacy talking/non-talking
history informs initial staff classification; ambiguous cases need staff review.
Scripts alone allows receiving ideas without imposing New Deal approval rules.
Scripts plus New Deal triggers the ongoing draft-review workflow.

An authorized staff message mentioning Scripts in #scripts announces a new assignment.
Send eligible creators a concise message with the script inline and draft-submission
action. Do not repeat first-video onboarding instructions for established creators.
New scripts replace unfinished assignments, including work awaiting review. Edits
update existing messages/cards without a fresh ping. Use #scripts as the source,
not the superseded script-library. Do not treat inspiration examples as assignments.

New Deal creators submit drafts in their creator channel and wait for approval
before posting. Legacy creators keep their existing approval arrangements. Draft
reviews belong in video-reviews; onboarding/account matters are separate.
Once approved, tell them they can publish. No published-link submission or automatic
post validation is required in this agreed new workflow. Earlier link-submission
instructions were superseded. Do not change historical reporting rules by inference.

## Platforms and day-to-day guidance

General publishing/account guidance covers TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.
A verified creator-specific staff exception takes precedence: do not claim all four
accounts are required, submitted or approved from a generic status-card template.
Facebook and YouTube are publishing destinations, not mandatory tracked metrics.
Do not invent missing Facebook/YouTube analytics or claim their posts were verified.
Older TikTok/Instagram tracking features do not imply new creators must submit links.

After first-post confirmation, request payment details through Payment hub in a
visible creator-channel message, not an ephemeral-only reply. Never ask for banking
details in public chat. General directory controls include Payment hub, My deal,
Creator stats, Request time off and appropriate staff controls. Keep directory copy
concise: current script state, trial end when applicable, team contacts and routine.
Time-off requests should include dates. Payment questions should identify the month.

## Shared resources

Creator Hub should consolidate team help, app access, assets, winning formats,
scripts and community without exposing new rates or rules to legacy creators.
Guides/resources are read-only for creators; Managers can publish. Community and
authorized legacy submission channels remain writable. Legacy-only resources stay
available to legacy creators and staff. Individual creator channels remain private
to their owner and authorized staff, not the entire Legacy role.

Owner approved a three-second app screenshot requirement, superseding old 1.5-second
guidance. The resource audit describes full-screen within the first ten seconds.
Read the current posting checklist for exact caption/bio wording; do not improvise.
Talking #yap guidance does not establish a universal rate. Old invoice tutorials
are superseded by no-manual-invoice guidance. Ad authorization remains a manual
private-channel process, not a new onboarding gate. Do not reuse stale QR codes.

App access should be simple, with separate iOS/Android instructions and native
Discord tutorial videos; only necessary installation links leave Discord. Link to
messages, not expiring signed attachment URLs. Preserve independent media backups.
Never claim a tutorial was watched or an entitlement was granted without evidence.
Do not advise a charge unless the intended test/no-charge flow is confirmed.

Do not revive historical factory-reset/proxy setup, staged comments, unsupported
health claims or invented personal results as standing instructions. Use genuine
viewer replies. Inspiration alerts are opt-in, not @everyone. Superseded material
should be archived and hidden with history preserved, not deleted.

## Facts to verify, not assume

Owner correction September 14: do not query the legacy database for creator
rankings, video counts, views or conversion analysis, including as a fallback.
Its historical Video records and "All Tracked Creators" import grouping are not
current performance evidence or a current program roster. Do not bypass this via
terminal/SQL access. Use Viral.app, or explicitly labeled beta tracker observations;
if neither covers the question, explain the missing coverage instead of using old
database counters. The legacy database still supports dedicated deal, identity and
payout tools; those financial workflows are separate from performance sourcing.

Owner update September 14: prefer Viral.app as the better video-data source for
now. The owned tracker is beta and is expected to replace it around September 24,
but that date is tentative (owner said "I believe"). Do not auto-switch by date or
claim replacement happened without a verified cutover. Use beta observations as
a labeled alternative when Viral lacks data; never silently mix their counters.
video_metrics provides selected public TikTok metrics and raw beta observations.
business_activity provides Superwall new-purchase/trial indices, not exact revenue.
Use these tools to investigate conversion questions rather than requiring the
creator to supply company data. An activity comparison is not causal attribution.
For a video comparison request the complete baseline plus target dates with
video_metrics(day, end_day) once, then business_activity for the same full window.
Compare the target with prior baseline days, excluding the target from the baseline.
Keep creator replies simple: daily view change, purchase/trial change, and what it
means. Avoid raw index jargon. Say "we cannot tell from this comparison" when
attribution is inconclusive, not "it did not convert" or a blanket claim of no evidence.
Other videos, paid activity and normal variation can explain business-wide changes;
if these were not checked, explicitly call the comparison preliminary. Public view
counts are not inherently secret; never combine them with private revenue ratios to
reconstruct company money. Do not query a one-day index as evidence of growth.

Exact individual rate, CPM, cap, bonus, cadence, qualifying-video threshold and payment
date come from the applicable agreement and verified records. The scripts workflow
file contains a two-distinct-videos-per-day note and a legacy threshold reference;
these are not independently established here as universal creator obligations.

## Spark Ads authorization reference

Owner clarification supplied September 30 from a September 29 creator conversation:
GoTall's intended authorization setup is at account level.

do not need to generate a separate authorization code for every new eligible public
video. Individual post-code authorization is a separate method; limited permissions,
expired/revoked access and specific posts can require further action.

and intended setup do not establish a particular creator's current connection or
payment eligibility, which depends on the applicable terms and verified records.
